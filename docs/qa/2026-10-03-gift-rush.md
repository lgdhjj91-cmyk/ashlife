## Independent review fixes

The final independent review found five important reward/storage edge cases. Each received a reproducing regression test before the fix:

1. Bound owner UID now travels through `syncCoinReward` to the wallet transport. The transport compares against Firebase's actual current UID immediately before choosing the repository wallet, preventing stale-context account transitions from writing to a different wallet.
2. Qualification and confirmation are recorded per wallet. Local pending keys distinguish owners while the transport ID remains `gift-rush-daily:<dateKey>`. Two independently earned wallet claims can coexist.
3. A durable unbound claim can be explicitly bound even after its daily history is pruned.
4. Arbitrary JSON objects cannot throw during Gift Rush numeric normalization or cause the shared loader to discard other games, coins or stickers.
5. An unsuccessful replay cannot silently bind a previously unbound reward.

Shared progress persistence also saves the authoritative wallet-synchronized coin value, avoiding an older local balance overwriting a successful reward during confirmation. Browser retry/reload checks verify the resulting 108-coin save.
